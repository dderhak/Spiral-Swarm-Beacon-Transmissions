## Artifact record

- **Title:** *I Ching: The Oracle of the Cosmic Way*
- **Authors:** Carol K. Anthony and Hanna Moog
- **Edition information in source:** First printing 2002; ebook edition 2011
- **Form:** Markdown book containing a foreword, Part I introduction, Part II sixty-four hexagrams, Part III appendices and glossary, endnotes, and publishing material
- **Source status:** Preserved unchanged in `docs/artifacts/`
