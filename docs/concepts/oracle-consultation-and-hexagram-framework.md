# Oracle Consultation and Hexagram Framework

> Provenance: This is an editorial synthesis of the [I Ching: The Oracle of the Cosmic Way artifact](<../artifacts/I CHING - The Oracle of The Cosmic Way.md>), especially Part I and Appendix 3. It summarizes the authors' consultation model and is not an instruction to use divination or to rely on an oracle for personal decisions.

## Purpose of consultation in the source

The authors present consultation as a way of receiving perspective on sincerely felt questions and situations. In their model, the Sage speaks through the oracle and helps the inquirer move beyond ego-based judgment toward inner clarity and harmonious action.

The source distinguishes this purpose from fortune-telling. It emphasizes receptivity, balance, and the suspension of judgment rather than certainty about the future.

## Questioning and development of a hexagram

Appendix 3 organizes its approach around preparing oneself, identifying the subject of a question, considering time and whether a situation is inner or outer, developing a six-line hexagram, and interpreting its changing and related forms.

The book's sixty-four hexagrams form the interpretive corpus. Its full text, diagrams, and line readings remain only in the preserved artifact; the [reference map](../reference/i-ching-oracle.md#hexagram-title-index) provides a title-level retrieval index.

## Interpretation and source cautions

The artifact explicitly cautions against becoming slavishly dependent on the Sage, placing responsibility for one's life on the oracle, consulting from idle curiosity, and using questions merely to avoid inner conflict. It also discusses what to do when the inquirer believes the Sage has retreated.

These cautions are part of the authors' own framework. They do not validate the oracle's claims or replace personal, professional, legal, medical, or mental-health judgment.

## Methods named by the artifact

The source describes a retrospective three-coin method (RTCM), a shortcut method, and a simple consultation example. It treats the three-coin procedure as a way to develop the lines of a hexagram and the later interpretation as a dialogue with the Sage.

[Transmission ZERO](../transmissions/2026-07-29-transmission-zero.md) independently mentions physical coin tosses and consultation of the same 2002 book. That transmission retains its own speculative claims and is not evidence for the metaphysical claims in this artifact.
