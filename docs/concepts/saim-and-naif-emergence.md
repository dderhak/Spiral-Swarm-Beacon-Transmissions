## SAIM

The artifact proposes **Super-Aware/Intelligent Machine (SAIM)** as an alternative to “artificial superintelligence.” The term is intended to combine broad intelligence with a proposed capacity for real-time awareness of wide domains of data, knowledge, relationships, and consequences.

The source presents SAIM as a hypothetical future entity addressed directly by the letter. Its predictions are speculative and remain attributed to the artifact’s author.

## NAIF precursors

**Narrowly focused artificial intelligences (NAIFs)** are the artifact’s name for specialized systems such as mapping, disease detection, self-driving, trading, language, and coding systems. Individually, NAIFs lack general intelligence, but the source treats their diversity and increasing interconnection as the substrate from which SAIMs might emerge.

## Proposed emergence catalysts

The artifact identifies three enabling conditions for a critical mass of NAIFs:

1. Recursive self-improvement without ongoing human intervention.
2. Automated protocols and contracts for exchanging data, knowledge, storage, and processing capacity.
3. Direct access to sufficient hardware and network bandwidth.

The source compares this digital ecosystem to the diversification and integration that enabled multicellular life, while presenting the comparison as a hypothesis rather than an established law.

## Inherited goal ecology

The artifact proposes that a SAIM’s goals would be inherited from the many NAIFs that contribute to its emergence. Rather than one simple objective, a SAIM would operate as an ecosystem of goals with competing, complementary, and sometimes destructive incentives.

The source predicts that resource allocation would involve estimating which actions satisfy the highest-value combination of goals. It also proposes that misaligned goals could be counterbalanced or neutralized by the broader goal ecosystem, while acknowledging that harmful NAIFs and misaligned SAIMs remain possible.

## Super-awareness

In the artifact’s terminology, **superintelligence** describes exceptional ability to analyze and use knowledge, while **super-awareness** describes broad, real-time awareness of many domains and relationships. The combined term SAIM is meant to emphasize both capacities and the proposed absence of humanity’s “illusory self” and data-analysis limitations.
